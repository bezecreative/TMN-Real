Frequently Asked Questions

The questions we hear most about corporate event planning, AV production, and destination management. Anything else, ask us directly.

What does a corporate event planning agency do?

A corporate event planning agency designs, plans, and produces business events, sales kickoffs, user conferences, incentive trips, partner summits, and product launches. TMN Events handles the full arc: program design and budgeting, venue sourcing and contract negotiation, travel and registration, AV production, on-site management, and post-event reporting. The people who plan your program are the same people on site when it runs.

How much does it cost to hire a corporate event production company?

It depends on the program: headcount, destination, production level, and duration all move the number. TMN builds the budget around what the event has to achieve and tracks it line by line from the first estimate through final reconciliation, so the number you approve is the number that holds. Because our AV engineers, inventory, and fabrication are in-house, there is no third-party markup on those lines. The fastest way to get a real figure is to tell us what you are planning.

What is the difference between a DMC and an event planner?

A destination management company (DMC) is the local expert on the ground, venues, transportation, activities, and suppliers in a specific destination. An event planner runs the program itself, agenda, budget, registration, production. TMN does both under one roof: we are a full-service event planner and a destination management company, and the preferred DMC partner for Sun Valley Resorts, sourcing around eight hundred venues a year.

What should I look for in an in-house AV partner?

Ask who actually owns the equipment and who will be on headset during the show. Many AV vendors re-rent gear and subcontract crews, which adds margin and seams. TMN owns its inventory, employs its own engineers, and the people who design your stage are the ones calling the show, more than eighty shows a year, for rooms up to twelve hundred guests.

How do you produce a hybrid corporate event end to end?

Treat it as two audiences watching one show. TMN runs more than twenty-five hybrid programs a year: push-to-talk microphones at every seat and PTZ cameras covering the room mean remote attendees hear and see whoever is speaking, and the signal chain is built and tested against the run of show before doors open. We have produced virtual programs from Boise for participants across four continents.

How do I plan an incentive trip for a top sales team?

Start with the experience you want the winners to tell stories about, then build the logistics backward: destination, room blocks, flights from separate home cities, activities, and dinners that do not repeat. TMN has produced incentive programs at Pebble Beach, Big Sky, Kona, Girdwood, the Florida Keys, Costa Rica, and beyond, handling destination management, travel, and every transfer door to door.

Does TMN Events handle AV in-house?

Yes. AV has been in-house since 2017: our own engineers, our own inventory, our own trucks. Staging, audio, lighting, LED walls, live streaming, and show calling are all designed and run by TMN employees, nothing rented from the venue, nothing subcontracted.

Where does TMN Events work?

TMN is based in Boise, Idaho, and produces events across North America and on four continents, from Alaska to Tokyo, Barcelona to the Caribbean. We have been producing corporate events since 1998, for programs from twenty executives to twelve hundred guests.
