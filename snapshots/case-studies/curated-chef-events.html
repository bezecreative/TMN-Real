The Challenge

Client entertainment that used to work, a suite at the game, standing around with a drink, no longer gets anyone talking. Prospects have seen it all before, and a sales team gets a handshake rather than a conversation.

The project required:

  • A format prospects hadn't been offered a dozen times already
  • A reason for senior people to give up an evening
  • A room built for conversation rather than spectating
  • Two to three uninterrupted hours between sales teams and prospects
  • Something to take away that outlasts the evening
